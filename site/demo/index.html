--- layout: page title: Try doorplate description: Type a US address and watch doorplate break it into parts, with the output it writes, on every keystroke. It runs in your browser. permalink: /demo/ ---

Type or paste a US address. doorplate reads it as you type, colors each part by what it decided the part is, and shows what it writes: the components, the USPS and RESO output, a match key and warnings. One line, any style; there are examples below the box.

The parser runs in your browser as WebAssembly. Nothing you type is sent anywhere. doorplate parses and formats addresses; it does not validate that an address exists or can receive mail.
